Questions

How do I pay?

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month to month. When a plan ends, your pages stay saved; anything beyond the free limits is unpublished until you upgrade again.

What happens to my RSVPs?

They're stored in your account. You can see them in your dashboard at any time, and Pro accounts can download them as a spreadsheet (CSV).

Can guests see my other projects?

No. Projects are private to your account. Guests only see the page you publish, at the link you share.

I'm a planner. Can I make pages for clients?

Yes, that's what Business is for. Make as many client pages as you need from one account.
